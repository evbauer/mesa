2. **“Relative” face metrics hide small-response errors.** `validate_table.py:108` divides by `max(1,|left|,|right|)`. Reproduction: force both adjacent cells at the tau face `(-9.685,7.92)`. Response-Jacobian jumps (`validate_table.py:114`) intentionally include response magnitude: these are field-scaled, not derivative-relative.
